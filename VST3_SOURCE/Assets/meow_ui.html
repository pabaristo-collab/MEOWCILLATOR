MEOWCILLATOR — QUICK START

1. PRESET: use the arrows to choose ARISTO MEOW, ACCORDION CAT, or other voices.

2. PLAY: click and drag inside the cat pad. Horizontal = pitch; vertical = expression. Turn ARP on to repeat notes. Turn HOLD on to keep playing after release.

3. DRUMS: choose a rhythm and press PLAY. Selecting a rhythm loads its suggested BPM; edit the number whenever you like. ARP and drums share tempo.

4. WAV: press REC WAV, play, then STOP REC and SAVE WAV. NEW TAKE discards the previous take. It does not record separate MIDI tracks.

5. CAT SOUNDS: tap MEOW, PURR, YOWL, HISS, CHIRP or GROWL independently of the touch synth/ARP/drums. These six pads play real recordings that you load individually. No recordings are bundled. Audio is included in WAV takes.

MIDI: connect a compatible keyboard in Chrome or Edge. This is the web edition, not a compiled VST3. Customize COLOR, MOTION STYLE and MOTION POWER below the pad. The screen updates live with your preset, drum style and BPM.
